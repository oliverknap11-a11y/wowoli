If something went wrong with a purchase, include your device model and roughly when it happened. That's usually enough for us to find it.

I paid and didn't get the skin

First, open Shop and tap Restore Purchases. This is free and works any number of times.

If a parent has to approve your purchases, the payment stays pending until they approve it. The skin unlocks on its own once they do — you don't need to pay again or reinstall.

If neither helps, email us and we'll sort it out.

I got a new phone

Paid skins are tied to your Apple ID. Install Wowoli, open Shop and tap Restore Purchases.

Coins, your best score and coin-unlocked skins live only on the device, so those don't carry over.

I want a refund

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through them at reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no access to payments and can't refund anything ourselves.

Stopping purchases on a child's phone

On the iPhone: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases. You can block in-app purchases entirely, or require approval for each one.

The game feels unfair

It shouldn't. The gap between obstacles always grows with speed so you keep the same time to react, and every gap is placed within reach of the last one. If you hit something that felt impossible, tell us what your score was — we tune this with real numbers.
