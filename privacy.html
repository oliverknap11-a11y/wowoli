Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Wowoli is a game. It doesn't need to know anything about you, so it doesn't ask. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and nowhere to enter personal details.

What stays on your device

These are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded and we cannot see them:

  • your best score and number of runs
  • coins you've collected
  • skins unlocked with coins
  • sound and vibration settings
  • your daily return streak

Purchases

Apple processes all payments. We never see your payment details or card number. To verify and restore purchases we use RevenueCat, which processes:

  • a randomly generated ID that is not linked to your name or email
  • your App Store purchase receipt (what was bought and when)
  • device model, OS version and store country

Without this there would be no way to make sure you keep what you paid for when you change phones. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold to anyone.

RevenueCat also adds these purchase records into sales statistics for us — how many copies of a given skin sold, for example. We see totals, never individual people.

What the game does not do

  • no advertising
  • no tracking across apps or websites
  • no analytics of how you play — no taps, screens or sessions are recorded
  • no accounts or sign-in
  • no servers of our own
  • no access to contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera

Children and teenagers

The game is rated 9+. Apart from the purchase records described above, the game collects nothing about anyone — and therefore nothing about children either. Parents can limit or completely disable in-app purchases in iPhone Settings under Screen Time.

Removing your data

Everything the game knows about you is on your phone. Deleting the app removes it. The game's settings also include Reset Progress, which clears it immediately. Records of paid purchases are held by Apple and RevenueCat — those exist so you don't lose what you paid for.

Changes

If what the game processes ever changes, this page and the date above change with it. Significant changes will be announced inside the app.
