What you're buying

Skins change how the character looks. Nothing else. On purpose:

  • a skin gives no advantage — the collision box is identical for all of them
  • a skin does not speed up progress or increase your score
  • the game is fully playable without a single purchase

Buying a skin gives you the right to use it in this game. It is permanent, with no further charges — no subscription, nothing to renew.

Coins

Coins are earned by playing only. They cannot be bought with money and have no value outside the game — they cannot be exchanged for money, transferred to another player, or moved to another app.

Restoring purchases

Paid skins are tied to your Apple ID. If you reinstall the game or get a new phone, you get them back with Restore Purchases in the shop or in settings. It's free and there's no limit on how many times you can use it.

Refunds

Apple processes payments, so refunds are requested directly from them at reportaproblem.apple.com. We have no access to payments and cannot issue refunds ourselves. If a purchase didn't reach your account, contact us — that we can fix.

Purchases by minors

If you are under 18, only make purchases with the permission of a parent or guardian. Parents can restrict or disable purchases in iPhone Settings under Screen Time, or require approval for every purchase.
