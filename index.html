WOWOLI

One tap. Zero gravity.

Flip gravity with a single tap. Run the neon tunnel, dodge everything, and see how far you get before it catches you.

How it works

  • One control. Tap anywhere to flip gravity. That's the whole game.
  • It never stops speeding up. But you always get a fair moment to react.
  • Collect skins. Earn them with coins, or get the rare ones.
  • No ads. No subscriptions. Ever.

Skins are whole characters

A skin isn't a recolour. Each one changes the silhouette — different visor, different boots, something in hand, and a different part of the body that glows. Some have three eyes. Some hover instead of walking.

Fair by design

Every skin has exactly the same collision box. Nothing you buy makes the game easier, faster or higher-scoring. The whole game is playable without spending anything.
